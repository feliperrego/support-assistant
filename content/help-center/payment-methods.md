All our prices are in US dollars.

## Ways to pay

We accept major credit and debit cards and Acme Outfitters gift cards.

## Gift cards

Acme Outfitters gift cards are digital. They never expire, and they can't be exchanged for cash.

## Keeping your card details safe

Our support team and our assistant never ask for your full card number. Never share it in a chat or an email.
