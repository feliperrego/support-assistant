---
title: Caring for your gear
category: Products and warranty
---

A little care keeps your gear working for many seasons. Always check the care label first.

## Tents

Let your tent dry completely before you pack it away, even after a dry night, to prevent mildew. Store it loosely in a cool, dry place.

## Rain jackets

Wash your rain jacket with a cleaner made for technical fabrics, and never use fabric softener, which clogs the fabric. Tumble dry it on low heat to restore its water repellency.

## Hiking boots

Brush off the mud after each hike and let your boots air dry at room temperature, away from heaters and campfires, which can crack the leather and weaken the glue.

## Sleeping bags

Store your sleeping bag loose in a large storage sack, not compressed in its stuff sack, so the insulation keeps its loft.
