Our clothing and footwear use standard US sizes, and each product page has its own size chart.

## Jackets

Our jackets fit true to size, with room for a light layer underneath. If you plan to wear a thick fleece under a rain jacket, choose one size up.

## Hiking boots

Our boots fit true to size with a regular hiking sock. If you wear thick socks or are between sizes, choose the larger size. Feet swell during the day, so measure yours in the evening.

## Still not sure?

The articles "Returns" and "Exchanges" explain what to do when a size doesn't work out.
