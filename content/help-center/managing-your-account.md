Your account keeps your orders, addresses and preferences in one place.

## Updating your details

Sign in and open Account settings to change your name, email address, saved addresses or email preferences. Changes take effect right away.

## What support can't change

Our support assistant never changes account details. Use Account settings, or the self-service link for a password, which the article "Resetting your password" explains.

## Your order history

Your account lists every order you have placed, with its status and tracking details.
