---
title: Resetting your password
category: Your account
---

You can reset your password yourself at any time.

## Send yourself a reset link

On the sign-in page, choose "Forgot password?" and enter the email address on your account. We email you a self-service link to choose a new password.

## If the email doesn't arrive

Check your spam or junk folder, and make sure you entered the email address you signed up with. You can ask for a new link at any time; only the newest link works.

## Why we can't reset it for you

For your security, the self-service link is the only way to reset a password. Our support assistant can't reset it for you.
