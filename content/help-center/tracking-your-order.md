---
title: Tracking your order
category: Shipping and delivery
---

You can follow every order from your account, and our support assistant can look up your orders for you.

## Order statuses

Each order shows one of these statuses:

- Processing: we have your order and are preparing it. It has not shipped yet.
- Shipped: your order is on its way. It has a tracking number and an estimated delivery date.
- Delivered: the carrier has marked your order as delivered.
- Returned: your return has arrived at our warehouse.

## Tracking number

When your order ships, we email you its tracking number and a link to follow the package. The same number and link appear in the order's details in your account.

## Estimated delivery date

Once your order ships, its details show an estimated delivery date, counted as the article "Shipping options and costs" explains.
